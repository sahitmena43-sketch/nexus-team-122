🎵 Music (nex!)

  • nex!play <song or URL> - Play
  • nex!skip - Skip
  • nex!stop - Stop
  • nex!pause - Pause
  • nex!resume - Resume
  • nex!volume <1-100> - Volume
  • nex!quit - Disconnect the bot from voice
  • nex!queue - View queue

24/7 & Loop

  • nex!247 - Toggle 24/7 mode
  • nex!loop <track/queue/off> - Loop mode

DJ & History

  • nex!setdj @role - Set DJ role
  • nex!history - Recent plays

Other

  • nex!ping - Bot status
  • nex!help - This menu
