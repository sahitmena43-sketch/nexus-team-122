Privacy Policy

NEXUS TEAM's privacy policy and data protection practices.

Last updated: October 8, 2026

1. Introduction

NEXUS TEAM takes your privacy and data security seriously. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that data.

By using NEXUS TEAM bots and services, you agree to the practices described in this document.

2. Data We Collect

NEXUS TEAM collects only the minimum data needed to operate its bots:

  • User ID: Your unique Discord account identifier
  • Username: Your Discord username
  • Server ID: The identifier of the server where a bot is used
  • Channel ID: The channel identifier for specific features
  • Role ID: Role identifiers for verification and management
  • Activity Data: Message and activity statistics used by statistics bots

3. How We Use Your Data

The data we collect is used exclusively to operate our bots:

  • Providing moderation and server management features
  • Creating statistics and leaderboards
  • Verifying users through Discord Linked Roles
  • Improving our services and adding new features
  • Troubleshooting technical issues and supporting users

4. Data Sharing

NEXUS TEAM does not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties.

Data may only be shared in the following circumstances:

  • When required by law or competent authorities
  • To protect the rights and security of NEXUS TEAM
  • With your explicit consent

5. Data Security

NEXUS TEAM uses strict security measures to protect your data:

  • Encrypted communication over HTTPS
  • Secure data storage on protected servers
  • Access limited to the NEXUS TEAM staff
  • Ongoing monitoring for suspicious activity
  • Regular security system updates

6. Data Retention

Your data is kept only for as long as needed to operate our services. When it is no longer needed, it is securely deleted.

You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting the NEXUS TEAM staff.

7. Your Rights

As a user of NEXUS TEAM services, you have the following rights:

  • Right of access: Learn what data is held about you
  • Right to correction: Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Right to deletion: Request deletion of your data
  • Right to restriction: Restrict the processing of your data
  • Right to object: Object to the use of your data

8. Children's Privacy

NEXUS TEAM services are not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under this age. If we discover that we have collected data from a child under 13, we will delete it immediately.

9. Policy Changes

NEXUS TEAM may update this Privacy Policy at any time. Changes will be published on this page with an updated date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

10. Contact

For questions, concerns, or requests about this Privacy Policy, contact the NEXUS TEAM staff through our official Discord server.
